Data lineage

OBSERVED: city population and country price/internet context come from dated Eurostat series. ESTIMATE: cost and ease remain estimated; comparative scores are normalised, designed for concept screening. They are not observed rents, footfall or a forecast. Revenue inputs are yours. Before committing to a site, replace city estimates with observed local data and street/premises evidence.

  • Eurostat City statistics - official city context and demographics.
  • Eurostat purchasing power parities - income/cost context.
  • OpenStreetMap - open premises and competitive-supply source for detailed follow-up.
  • DataForSEO Trends API - search-demand data route for production.
  • Location Potential Europe - street-level follow-up where available.
Decision support, not a final decision. Verify current requirements and source data before committing resources.
